SOLMAR BAY is an original open-world crime game in the browser: you are Milo Vane, a drifter with $120 and a borrowed jacket, working for Rosa Marlin, the fixer who runs the marina. Explore a sun-soaked procedural city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the Bay Police under a 1–5 star wanted system, fight on foot, and run three chained story missions — steal the marina coupe, move a crate for the Neon Palms hotel, and crack the Bay Credit vault — plus taxi fares and golden-shell collectibles on the side. Third-person camera, traffic and pedestrians, a live minimap, health/cash/wanted HUD, synthesised WebAudio engine hum, sirens and music, and localStorage saves. Everything is original — own city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art and sound.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network, just static files under game/.
